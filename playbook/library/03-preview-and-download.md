## Overview

The Documents library lets members open supported files in the browser or download copies for offline use. Permissions follow the same rules as viewing the folder.

## Preview in browser

1. Open **Documents** and navigate to the file.
2. Click the document name or **Preview** action.
3. Review the content in the preview modal. Close the modal when finished.

Preview supports common office and image formats configured by your administrator. Unsupported types can still be downloaded.

## Download

1. Locate the document in its folder.
2. Click **Download** to save the file to your device.

Downloaded copies are your responsibility to store securely when they contain sensitive {{ entity_label }} information.

## Linked documents

Other packages — Meetings, Bylaws, Insurance, and Finances — may link directly to documents stored in the library. Those links respect the same access rules as browsing Documents directly.
