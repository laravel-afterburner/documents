## Overview

Documents are grouped in folders so council, staff, and owners can find records quickly without cluttering the root library.

## Steps

1. Open **Documents** from the main navigation for your current {{ entity_label }}.
2. Browse the folder tree on the left (or top-level list, depending on layout).
3. Click a folder to view its files.
4. Use search or filters when available to locate a specific document.

## Permissions

Folder visibility follows your role and {{ entity_label }} document settings. If you cannot see a folder, contact an administrator with **update** access on the {{ entity_label }}.
